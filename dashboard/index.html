MISS-ME

My Intelligent Sentry Secures Me — it does its best work offline.

SLEEPING The sentry is quiet. Nothing has knocked.
Engine: on-device scorer ready Cloud: not configured — running fully on-device Alerts: break through Do Not Disturb. Only airplane mode silences the sentry.

Nothing to steal

What MISS-ME holds: nothing worth taking. A sentry that holds your passwords is a vault a thief can rob. This one holds none of it.

  • No passwords. It never asks for one, never stores one, and never reads what you type — it only notices when a page asks you to type one somewhere unsafe.
  • No accounts. No payment details. There is no account to break into and no card on file.
  • Your names never leave this device. Watched names live in this browser only. A message carrying one never goes to the cloud.
  • The cloud only ever sees an uncertain message — never a watched name — and only sends back a short verdict and a remedy.
  • The event log lives in this browser only — and you can clear it any time.

Don’t take our word for it — storm the gate and see what a thief would find.

Name Watch — setup

List the people the sentry guards — your name in every variation, and anyone else you protect: family, clients. The roster holds 50 people at a time, and if someone tries to get into any of their information, it gets flagged. The sentry will also suggest a cover name to keep your information under.

Name Watch lives on this device only. Your variants are stored in this browser and never leave it — they are never sent to the cloud, and a message carrying a watched name is never sent to the cloud either. And they are not stored plain, or whole: at setup the app breaks your names into syllables and mixes them in with decoy syllables, each at its own binary position on the lattice, so anyone who opens the store sees fragments and cannot tell which are yours.

        How the sentry decides

        1. On-device scorer — instant, free, private. Clear threats (score 70+) and clear safe messages (score 20 or less) settle here. Nothing leaves the device.
        2. On-device model — Gemini Nano in Chrome, only when the score is in between. A confident verdict here still never leaves the device.
        3. Cloud check — Firebase AI Logic (Gemini in the cloud), only when the on-device verdict is still uncertain, and only when a Firebase config is filled in. The cloud does no heavy lifting: it sends back a short verdict and a suggested remedy. What reaches you is the notification and the remedy — a light in the dark, a sound that breaks through Do Not Disturb. Then it sleeps again.

        No cloud config? The sentry still works — uncertain events stay uncertain and are flagged for you, never silently passed.
